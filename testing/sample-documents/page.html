Project Falcon — Launch Report

Summary: Falcon reached 1,017 active users and a 96.2% document-attachment success rate during the Q3 beta.

1. Adoption

Weekly active users grew 34% week-over-week after the beta invite wave. The top three retention drivers were voice mode, document attachments, and custom agent modes.

2. Document attachments

Users attached PDFs, spreadsheets, presentations, and plain-text files directly in chat. The model answered questions over the extracted text, including page counts for PDFs and per-sheet rows for spreadsheets.

The most common failures were legacy .doc / .xls files (rejected with a clear message) and a small number of corrupt PDFs.

3. Known issues

  • Very large files (>20 MB) are rejected with an explanatory error.
  • Extracted text over 40,000 characters is truncated with a visible note.
  • Only one attachment kind (image or document) is allowed per message.

4. Next steps

Roll the share-sheet integration to production, add multi-attachment support, and instrument attachment success metrics per format.
