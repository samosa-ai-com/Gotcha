OpenWakeWord Model Notices
==========================

This product includes wake-word detection models from the OpenWakeWord
project (https://github.com/dscripka/openWakeWord).

Models bundled in app/src/main/assets/openwakeword/
---------------------------------------------

  melspectrogram.onnx
    Onnx export of the OpenWakeWord melspectrogram front-end.
    Source: https://github.com/dscripka/openWakeWord (v0.5.1 release assets).
    License: Apache License 2.0.

  embedding_model.onnx
    Re-implementation of Google's speech embedding model
    (https://tfhub.dev/google/speech_embedding/1), as used by OpenWakeWord.
    Source: https://github.com/dscripka/openWakeWord (v0.5.1 release assets).
    License: Apache License 2.0.

  hey_gotcha.onnx
    Custom "Hey Gotcha" classifier head, trained by the Gotcha project on
    synthetic positive clips (Piper TTS) and OpenWakeWord's standard
    negative/background datasets (ACAV100M, AudioSet, MIT RIRs, FMA music).
    Architecture: small fully-connected classifier on top of the frozen
    speech embedding backbone.
    License: Apache License 2.0. Trained by Gotcha contributors.

The Apache 2.0 license text is available at:
