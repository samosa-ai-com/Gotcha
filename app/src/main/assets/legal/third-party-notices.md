## Not bundled: Gotcha Shell's Linux runtime

Gotcha Shell, the companion app that provides the Linux shell, does **not**
ship its runtime in its APK. PRoot (GPL-2.0-only), talloc
(LGPL-3.0-or-later) and libandroid-shmem (BSD-3-Clause) are downloaded once,
on first use, and run as a separate process inside Gotcha Shell. Their
licence texts are installed next to the binaries, together with a pointer to
their complete corresponding source, so they appear in Gotcha Shell rather
than here.
